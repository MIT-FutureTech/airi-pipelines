# Risk classification

**Work in progress**

Each extracted risk is classified according to [AIRI's taxonomies](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1wxg-hZAjGvFHcsfnEp1KAJJo5xvf98MB2v50B5URXZM/) to organize the risks under shared frameworks.

## Links

- [risk_repository/classify.py](https://github.com/MIT-FutureTech/airi-pipelines/tree/main/pipelines/risk-repository/src/risk_repository/classify.py): draft implementation

## Inputs

- The `ExtractedRisk` entries produced by the <NodeLink id="rr-risk-extraction">risk extraction</NodeLink> stage, containing the authors' category, subcategory and description, along with a supporting quote from the paper..

## Outputs

- Per-document JSON in `output/classify/` containing one `ClassifiedRisk` per extracted risk

## Causal taxonomy

Three independent categories, one value each:

| Category | Values |
|----------|--------|
| Entity: what causes the risk | human, AI, other |
| Intent: expected vs unexpected outcome | intentional, unintentional, other |
| Timing: stage in the AI lifecycle | pre-deployment, post-deployment, other |
